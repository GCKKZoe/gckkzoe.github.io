Research Interests

My research examines how information frictions shape investor decisions and market discipline in financial intermediation. I study how disclosure design affects investors' responses to mutual fund fees and how analysts' incentives and capacity constraints shape the information they produce. These projects examine both the information investors receive and their ability to use it to evaluate financial intermediaries.
